1. Check the company's sick leave policy: The policy should be available on the company's website or in the employee handbook.

2. Contact HR: If the policy is not available, the employee should contact HR to inquire about the sick leave policy.

3. Provide the necessary information: The employee should provide their name, job title, and the reason for the sick leave.

4. Provide the necessary documentation: If the employee is unable to provide the necessary documentation, they should provide a doctor's note or a self-certification form
